Power system:
1. Verify battery charging capabilities
    Process: Connect PV to battery, record voltage at different times
    Success: Battery voltage increases as solar panel illuminated
2. Measure Pi current draw
    Process: Measure current through Pi when connected to power
    Success: Current does not exceed 3A
3. Battery runtime test
    Process: Fully charge batter, disconnect solar panel. Run system continually & measure battery voltage throughout the day
    Success: Battery can sustain system overnight
4. Worst-Case test
    Process: Manually trigger flood mode and measure battery voltage, current draw, and temperatures at different times
    Success: Device functional for upward of 6 hours
5. Solar recharge test
    Process: Discharge battery, connect panel, measure battery voltage throughout the day
    Success: Battery recovers voltage
6. Buck converter voltage stability
    Process: Connect to battery, measure voltages across 5V and 12V rails while sensors activated
    Success: Voltage remains in a range of +/- 0.2 V of desired
